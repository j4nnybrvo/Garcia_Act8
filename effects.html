THE EFFECTS OF GAMING

How Mobile Games Influence Our Daily Lives

The Positive Effects

One reason people enjoy mobile games is that they give us something fun to do when we need a break. They can make difficult days feel a little lighter and allow us to spend time with friends, even when we are far away from each other.

  • Relaxation: Games can help us take a break from everyday stress.
  • Friendship: Multiplayer games help us meet and communicate with others.
  • Problem-solving: Challenges encourage us to think carefully.
  • Entertainment: Games make our free time more enjoyable.

Gaming can be a positive part of our lives when we know how to manage our time and responsibilities.

The Negative Effects

Problems can begin when gaming takes up too much of our time. We might delay our schoolwork, lose sleep, or spend less time with the people around us. Sometimes, we may also turn to games whenever we feel sad instead of finding healthy ways to deal with our feelings.

  • School responsibilities may be neglected.
  • Too much screen time can affect our sleep routine.
  • Face-to-face conversations may become less frequent.
  • Problems may remain unresolved when we always avoid them.

Online Friends and Real-Life Relationships

Online friendships can be meaningful. It feels good to play with people who understand us, listen to us, and make us feel included. For some people, these friendships provide comfort during difficult times.

However, relationships outside the game matter, too. Spending time with family, talking with trusted friends, and joining activities can help us feel connected to the world beyond the screen.

The goal is not to choose between online and real life. It is to make room for both.
